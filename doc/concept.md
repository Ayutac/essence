## Techniques

The simplest technique is a ball of Essence and make it fly where you want. If it hits an entity, 
does 1.0 damage and dissipates. If it doesn't hit an entity, it simply stops after a while (no gravity),
obstructs, stays in place. Because of your object permanence you can only have a couple of balls out at
a time, and if you summon an additional one, the first vanishes.
You can recall a ball to YOU, and it still does its damage thing if it hits something underway.

The second-simplest one is the pillar: You can shoot out an obstructing (vertical) pillar (which hurts)
or you can aim it at the ground to propel yourself up.

Other techniques that go the "Essence Entity" route:
* Spikes
* Waves
* Bisecting

Utility techniques:
* Spring step (for increased step height)
* One Piece Moonwalk just with Essence slabs
* half-transformation to fly upwards/reduce fall damage
* full transformation to be immune to most damages
